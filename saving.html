Saving With Us

Whatever you're saving for, opening an account with Dragonsavers is the first step to reaching your savings goal!

Members all save together - so you'll be in good company.

The minimum deposit each time you save is just $1 and you need to keep at least $5 in your account to keep it open. You can save in cash at our branch, by making deposits at an ATM, by texting a photo of a check, or by direct deposit of any benefits or hrough payroll deduction if this is supported by your employer.

All savings accounts are instant access and the minimum withdrawal is $10. Withdrawals can be made online through the Members Area, at an ATM, or at our branch.

Members are encouraged to take a loan secured against their savings rather than withdraw large amounts. Interest rates are low for secure loans and you keep your balance.

Anyone over 18 who attends or works at Drexel, or lives in the same house as an existing member can apply for Dragonsavers Credit Union membership. You will need a copy of an official document with your current address dated in the last three months, and a copy of your Drexel photo ID with your membership application. Accounts are free to open. The annual membership fee of $2 is debited every October.

The credit union pays dividends on your savings rather than a bank charging you fees! A dividend is a percentage that the membership votes on to be paid back to the members. That percentage will be multiplied by your average daily balance over a six month period then deposited to your account.

A dividend is paid to all regularly saving members (a minimum of at least once every six months) after the Annual General Meeting. Members attending the Annual General Meeting vote on the level (percentage) of dividend for the previous year.

JOIN & SAVE

Junior deposit accounts are available for those under 18. Junior deposit accounts are free to open and there is no annual subscription. Interest is paid annually on the 30th of September.

Community accounts are available for Businesses, Project Groups, Trusts, Clubs, Societies, Associations and Charities. The annual membership fee of $2 is debited every October.

Contact us for more information or for a junior or community application form
