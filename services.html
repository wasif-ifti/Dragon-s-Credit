Special Offers

Refer a Member

Refer a friend or family member to recieve a $5 bonus. The refered member must be active for 3 months before your account will be credited with $5. There is no limit to how many new members you can refer!

Groups and Associations Refer a Member

Any group or association with a membership account with us can also earn $5 per member referral. Please encourage your organizations members to become members of the credit union. Your account will be credited with $5 per member after they have been active for 3 months. There is no limit to how many referrals you can earn $5 for! Consider it a fundraiser!

Payroll Loans

Our members can qualify for an instant $500 payroll loan as long as your pay is automatically deposited to your account at the credit union. Your payment of 12% of the loan value will be automatically deducted from your first payroll deposits of the month. For a loan of $500 that is $60 per month. You may make additional payments to a payroll loan to pay it off faster. Only one payroll loan per member at any time.
