Borrowing From Us

Are you looking to borrow? - we are committed to:

  • Making available, when needed, low cost credit
  • Helping you to save for future needs
  • Being approachable
  • Helping to keep your level of debt under control
  • Offering free insurance protection on savings and loans
  • Offering flexibility for savings and loans

PLEASE REMEMBER: Loans are made from other members savings, DO NOT apply if you can't afford to repay these members their savings back again.

Truth-in-lending, with no hidden costs and no penalty for early repayment is the policy of the credit union movement. Members are encouraged to continue saving particularly while repaying a loan.

Why Choose a Credit Union Loan?

  • Free Insurance Protection
  • Low Interest Rates
  • No Early Repayment Penalty
  • No Hidden Fees
  • Flexible Repayment Terms
